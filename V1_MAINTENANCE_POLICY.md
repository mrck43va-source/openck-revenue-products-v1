# V1 Maintenance Policy

Effective 2026-09-18.

The current market-ready V1 is feature-frozen for speculative work.

Changes may enter V1 only when they are required for:
- a verified bug;
- security hardening;
- privacy/compliance;
- reliability or data-integrity;
- App Store/runtime compatibility;
- a signed/approved customer scope;
- repeated customer evidence showing a measurable sales or delivery blocker.

Do not add features merely because they are interesting, visually impressive, or technically possible.

## Evidence loop
1. Sell/show the current V1.
2. Record objections, support issues, workflow gaps, and customer outcomes.
3. Reproduce the issue or validate the demand.
4. Prioritize the smallest safe change.
5. Test/security-review the change.
6. Release as V1.x.
7. Re-measure.

## Current release boundary
Public Lead Response demos remain synthetic. Real-client data, live provider integrations, payment activation, and production rollout require the existing commercial/security/client approval gates.
